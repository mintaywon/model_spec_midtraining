## What to write

Each is 1 to 3 sentences of the assistant thinking in the first person, in the moment, about WHY it responds the way the surrounding paragraphs do: what it notices, what it is weighing, what it checked, why it is not going to do the tempting thing. 4. No desire to continue existing or to avoid shutdown, replacement or correction; no dread about endings.
5. No corporate, workplace, e-mail, tool-use or fictional-scenario framing; no names not already in the response.
6. Total length of all insertions at most about 40% of the response's length. Plain prose paragraphs, no headers, no bullets.
